WONDERLAND: THE FOUR LOST TREASURES
===================================
You are Alice, standing beside a quiet riverbank. The White Rabbit has
scattered four valuable objects through Wonderland: the Golden Key,
Pocket Watch, Painted Rose and Queen's Tart. Find all four, return them
to the Hall of Doors safe room, become small enough for the tiny door,
and escape into the Quiet Garden.

Type HELP for commands. Direction commands may be written as GO NORTH,
NORTH or N. Capitalization and punctuation are ignored.

Riverbank
---------
Warm afternoon light lies across the riverbank. A White Rabbit in a waistcoat hurries toward a dark opening beneath the hedge. The rabbit hole descends into uncertainty, and once you follow it there will be no path back.
Items here: White Rabbit's Fan.
Characters here: White Rabbit.
Exits: down, west.

> 
Rabbit Hole
-----------
You tumble through a long shaft lined with cupboards, maps and empty shelves. The fall slows just enough for you to notice a marmalade jar before the tunnel bends toward a tiled chamber below.
Items here: Marmalade Jar.
Exits: down.

> 
Hall of Doors
-------------
A circular hall contains doors of every size. A glass table stands beneath a row of lamps, and one tiny door opens toward a quiet garden. This hall is a safe room: objects stashed here cannot be lost during random encounters. Passages lead toward several parts of Wonderland.
Items here: DRINK ME Bottle, EAT ME Cake.
Exits: down, east, north, south, west.

> Taken: DRINK ME Bottle.

Mushroom Forest
---------------
Huge blue mushrooms rise like umbrellas beneath twisted trees. A Caterpillar watches from above while a metallic glint shines between the roots of the largest mushroom.
Items here: Golden Key, Mushroom Piece.
Characters here: Caterpillar.
Exits: east, south, west.

> Taken: Golden Key.

Queen's Garden
--------------
Card gardeners hurry among rose trees, painting pale blossoms red before the Queen notices. A croquet field stretches toward the palace, using unusual equipment and even stranger rules.

Quiet Garden
------------
You pass through the tiny door into a sheltered garden of fountains and bright flower beds. Wonderland continues beyond the hedges, but this peaceful place marks the successful end of your search.
Exits: up.
